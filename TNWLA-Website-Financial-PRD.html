1. Purpose of This Document

This document sets out the complete commercial position for the design, development and deployment of the official website for the Tamilnadu Women Law Association — Madras (TN Govt Reg: 194/2023), together with its legal wing, Stand Firm Legal Associates. It states plainly what has been built, what it cost to build, what is payable, and what additional third-party costs the Association must budget for separately.

Nothing in this quotation is hidden or conditional. The one-time development fee is ₹25,000. Domain, hosting and email are not included in that figure and are addressed directly in Section 7.

2. Scope of Work Delivered

The following has been designed, built, tested and deployed as a single production website:

2.1 Cinematic front end

  • Scroll-linked hero cinematography — the visitor's scroll drives video playback frame by frame, forwards and in reverse, rather than a passive autoplay loop.
  • Source footage processed and re-encoded with dense keyframes for smooth frame seeking; watermark removal and closing freeze-frame extraction.
  • Luxury design system: serif/sans typographic pairing, gold gradient treatment, glass panels, cursor glow, particle field and motion reveals throughout.
  • Light and dark theme with visitor preference remembered between visits.

2.2 Content architecture

  • Practice Areas — 9 categories of cases undertaken, each with descriptive copy.
  • Property E-Services — 9 services presented as an animated tile grid.
  • Registrations & Online Services — 13 business and personal registrations on a vertical gold timeline.
  • About, Statistics, Client Testimonials, Legal Updates (blog), FAQ accordion.
  • Gallery — dedicated inner page, 36-frame flip wall with full-size lightbox viewing.

2.3 Digital forms engine

  • TNWLA membership application as a guided multi-step wizard with declaration, consent capture and post-submission delivery confirmation.
  • 26 individual deed preparation request forms, each with its own field set.
  • Formal document preview, one-click PDF generation, and dispatch via WhatsApp or Gmail to the Association's desk — with no server or database dependency.

2.4 Bilingual delivery

  • Complete English and Tamil translation across every section, form field, service name and message, with instant switching and no page reload.

2.5 Engagement and discovery

  • Contact suite: Google Map embed, enquiry form, live appointment date and time-slot picker, drag-and-drop document upload with previews.
  • Assistive chatbot, full-site search overlay, floating call and WhatsApp actions.
  • Search engine optimisation: metadata, OpenGraph cards, LegalService structured data, sitemap and robots configuration.
  • Fully responsive across mobile, tablet and desktop; deployed as a static export for fast load and low running cost.
